### On-site display

Use the allocated poster boards near our workshop room, **MR C3.6**. Do not attach posters to venue walls. Use the venue-approved adhesive strips provided in the workshop room, one per corner, and remove your poster after your session. Further board details will be announced.

### Printing and collection

Bring your printed poster or use the optional [NeurIPS Sydney workshop printing service](https://www.postersessiononline.eu/pr/poster2.asp?congreso=469631373). You may use any printer.

Partner-printed posters can be collected at **Exhibition Centre, Level 2, Registration Desk 3**. Saturday collection hours are **07:00–15:30**.

Check the [NeurIPS printing instructions](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/PosterInstructions) and the printing service for current ordering deadlines before placing an order.

### Digital poster

[Upload your virtual poster and paper thumbnail to NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/PosterUpload). NeurIPS recommends uploading at least one week before the conference; no fixed upload deadline is listed.
